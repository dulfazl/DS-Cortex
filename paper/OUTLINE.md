# Paper outline

Working title: **DS Cortex: Evidence-Grounded Deliberation for Multi-Agent Data Science**
(alternative: *Debate, Verify, Remember: A Multi-Agent Data Science System with Calibrated Confidence*).

Every number in the paper comes from `reports/eval/`, regenerated by `python -m eval.run_all --seeds 5`.
Write Results, the Introduction and the Abstract only after the final, frozen run.

## Sections (8-page two-column budget)

1. **Abstract** (about 200 words): problem, three pillars, setup (9 datasets, 6 configs, 5 seeds), headline numbers, one limitation.
2. **Introduction** (1 page): ungrounded numbers, post-hoc metric choice, silent decisions, missed leakage. Contributions:
   C1 evidence-gated debate with a rule-based resolution table; C2 confidence computed from experiments, with a
   calibration analysis; C3 critic-gated fingerprint memory; C4 an evaluation protocol (flaw injection, ablations,
   hallucinated-claim rate). The router, dashboard and export are infrastructure, not contributions.
3. **Related work** (0.75 page): AutoML, LLM agents for data science, multi-agent debate, calibration, leakage.
4. **System overview** (0.75 page): architecture figure, Decision Ledger, run modes, tools compute / LLM narrates.
5. **Method** (2 pages): 5.1 deliberation (`workflows/debate_graph.py`, `workflows/resolution_table.py`, verifier
   `tools/verify_tools.py`); 5.2 evidence and confidence (`tools/sklearn_tools.py::evidence_confidence`:
   0.3 stability + 0.4 bootstrap separation + 0.3 effect over baseline); 5.3 memory (`tools/fingerprint_tools.py`,
   `memory/fingerprint_store.py`, distance threshold 0.35, critic gating).
6. **Experimental setup** (1 page): datasets, six injected flaws, configs A to F, metrics, seeds, tests.
7. **Results** (1.5 pages): one subsection per RQ, negative results included.
8. **Case study** (0.5 page): the leakage blocker and the client-override flow, with a ledger excerpt.
9. **Discussion and limitations** (0.5 page): threats to validity below.
10. **Conclusion and future work**: temperature scaling, a learned resolution policy, live monitoring, debate across different LLMs.
11. **Ethics and reproducibility**: dataset licences (`reports/eval/datasets.csv`), upload privacy, code release with DOI, one command.
12. **Appendix**: prompts (`prompts/`), resolution table, fingerprint features, confidence formula, per-dataset results.

## Threats to validity

- Nine datasets: results are indicative, not general.
- One model family plays every agent, so debaters may share blind spots; in offline mode the positions are
  rule-based and the hallucinated-claim rate measures the rules, not an LLM.
- Public benchmark datasets may be in the model's training data.
- Injected flaws are synthetic; the label-noise detector also fires on hard clean datasets (see `false_alarms.csv`).
- The confidence formula and the resolution table are hand-designed.
- Rows are capped at 3,000 and the evaluation uses fast settings (5-fold CV, 1 repeat, 8 tuning trials).
- Free-tier rate limits inflate run times; call and token counts are exact.
- The drift check is a two-sample test, not monitoring.
